Readers stay with video longer on economy and demographics stories than on any other topic in the spreadsheet.

What to do next

Lean into the proven beats
Prioritize crime/policing, economy/demographics, immigration and housing when you need reliable audience returns.
Use timing strategically
Morning is the safest bet; test 11 a.m. and just after midnight for stories with breakout potential.
Match packaging to goal
Daily for new/mobile reach, enterprise for desktop depth, quick-turn enterprise for loyal repeat readers.
